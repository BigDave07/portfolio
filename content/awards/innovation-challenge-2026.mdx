## The challenge

Innovation Challenge 2026 at the University of Regina asked one big question: how do we
revitalize downtown Regina?

## Our idea

Our solution, the Downtown Unity Centre, is a central hub for community connection,
employment pathways and entrepreneurship, built to bring real energy back to the city core.
We placed second out of the teams that pitched. Thank you to the organizers, mentors and
judges who made it possible.
