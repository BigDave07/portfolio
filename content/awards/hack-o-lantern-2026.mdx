## The idea

For Hack O Lantern I carved a pumpkin and gave it a personality: the Watch-o-Lantern.

## How it works

The pumpkin is wired to an Arduino with lights, a speaker and a webcam. When people talk to
it, it answers back and roasts them.

## The setup

The code runs on my laptop next to the pumpkin, and a hand-drawn sign carries its name.
