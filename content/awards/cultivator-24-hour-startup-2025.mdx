## The event

The 24 Hour Startup, run by Cultivator in Regina, brings together developers, designers
and entrepreneurs from across Saskatchewan and beyond. Over 24 hours my team, Penrider,
brainstormed, built, broke, rebuilt and finally pitched our MVP.

## The prizes

- **$5,000 in in-kind legal services** from McKercher LLP, in partnership with Cultivator.
- **$2,000** from Conexus Credit Union, on a cheque marked "First Place".

Both were awarded to Penrider on November 29, 2025. Thank you to Cultivator, the mentors,
the judges and the other teams we worked alongside.
