## The event

The 24 Hour Startup, run by Cultivator in Regina, took place during Agribition and brought
together developers, designers and entrepreneurs from across Saskatchewan and beyond. Over
24 hours my team, Penrider, brainstormed, built, broke, rebuilt and finally pitched our MVP.

## The problem

We started by asking people at the event, farmers especially, what problems they run into.
One stood out: keeping track of cattle means walking among hundreds of cows and writing
notes by hand.

## Penrider

Penrider is our answer: a way for farmers to keep track of their cows without going around
hundreds of animals with a notebook.

## The prizes

We won first place overall, with two prizes worth $7,000 in total, both awarded to Penrider
on November 29, 2025:

- **$5,000 in in-kind legal services** from McKercher LLP, in partnership with Cultivator.
- **$2,000** from Conexus Credit Union, on a cheque marked "First Place".

Thank you to Cultivator, the mentors, the judges and the other teams we worked alongside.
