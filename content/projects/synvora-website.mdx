## What it is

The public face of Synvora: a cinematic marketing site that opens with an SVG
stroke-draw brand intro, plus a waitlist where people can see their referral position,
backed by Supabase.

## How it's built

- Next.js 14 and Tailwind, with GSAP, Lenis and Framer Motion for motion and smooth scrolling.
- A full SEO pass: the Next.js Metadata API, JSON-LD structured data and Core Web Vitals.
