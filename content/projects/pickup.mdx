## The problem

I ran 30+ customer discovery calls with businesses in Regina, Saskatoon, Winnipeg,
Calgary and Edmonton. One failure kept coming up: leads from paid search die at
phone intake, because nobody answers or follows up in time. Two law firms confirmed
it independently.

## What I built

- A working voice agent demo that qualifies inbound callers and books consultations straight into the firm's calendar, deployed on Vercel with Google Cloud telephony.
- A missed-call recovery MVP on Twilio, the Claude API, Supabase and a Next.js dashboard.

## Where it's going

After benchmarking against Vapi and Retell AI, I moved Pickup from per-client builds
to a multi-tenant, self-serve platform. I also run the outreach and handle technical
pre-sales conversations with non-technical business owners.
