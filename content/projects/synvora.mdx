## The product

Synvora is a consumer skincare app for iOS and web. You scan your face from three
angles, and it gives you observations and a routine built from products you can
actually buy in Canada. It's live on the App Store.

## What I built

I built the whole product as the technical cofounder:

- A React Native (Expo, TypeScript) iOS app with 39+ screens and subscription billing through RevenueCat.
- A Next.js marketing site.
- A multi-stage LLM backend on Supabase Edge Functions: a three-angle vision scan, a cached verdict layer, a routine assembler, a safety gate, and a conversational coach that remembers prior scans and onboarding answers.

## Decisions that mattered

- **Routing by task to cut cost.** Claude Sonnet handles vision analysis and product verdicts; Haiku handles chat. Verdicts are cached against a curated product database instead of re-querying per user.
- **Observations, not diagnosis.** The model is constrained to describe visible traits only. That product-safety rule shaped the prompt contract and the App Store health-content rating.
- **A curated product database.** 108 Canada-available products with metadata like fragrance-free flags, ingredient conflicts and pregnancy cautions, merged and cleaned from Open Beauty Facts and enriched with the Claude API.
